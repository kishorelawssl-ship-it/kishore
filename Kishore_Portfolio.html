About

I study how law, public policy and technology shape everyday life in Tamil Nadu and India.

I am a law student at Saveetha School of Law with a Maths and Computer Science background from school. I write on social justice, health governance, the judiciary, climate policy and caste, and I co-author technology papers published by IEEE.

I present at national and international conferences and help organise them.
